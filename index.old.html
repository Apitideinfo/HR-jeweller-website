Our Legacy

The Story of HR Jewellers & Sons

Over seven decades of absolute purity, authenticity, and generational relationship building.

A Legacy of Pure Gold Since 1952

Founded by Shri Hariramji in 1952, HR Jewellers & Sons started as a modest design atelier committed to uncompromising purity. By adhering strictly to traditional hand-forged methods and high moral business ideals, we earned the trust of generations of families.

Today, the third generation carries this heritage forward, combining classical royal aesthetics with modern hallmarked precision. We maintain our focus on providing custom showroom previews and offline services, keeping each client relationship personal and secure.

1952
Showroom Inception

Shri Hariramji establishes the first flagship showroom, specializing in bespoke hand-beaten gold ornaments.

1985
Generation Expansion

Expanding our inventory to include royal Kundan ornaments and heavy sterling silver articles for temple shrines.

2005
Hallmark Certification Integration

Among the initial regional jewellers to integrate 100% BIS Hallmark guidelines, ensuring complete customer transparency.
